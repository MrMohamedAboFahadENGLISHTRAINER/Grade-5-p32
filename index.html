Read & Listen: Kids' Health
Kids' Health by Dr. Gary Potts

Not feeling well? Here are some things you can do before you visit your doctor, dentist or pharmacist!

🤕 Headache

Put some ice in a towel and put it on your head. Relax in the dark for about half an hour. Sometimes our head hurts because of a toothache or an earache so call the doctor when your headache doesn't stop!

🥤 Sore Throat

You should drink warm water with honey and lemon juice. Mix two tablespoons of honey and two tablespoons of lemon juice in a cup, then add warm water. You shouldn't drink cold water when you have a sore throat!

🦷 Toothache

You can mix water and some salt in a glass. Rinse your mouth with that for a minute, but don't swallow it. Also, you shouldn't eat desserts when you have a toothache!

⚠️ Remember! You shouldn't take medicine without asking a doctor first!
